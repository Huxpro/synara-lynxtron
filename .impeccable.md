## Design Context

### Users

Synara is for software developers who run coding agents across multiple local
projects and long-lived threads. They use it as a focused workbench for
starting, supervising, and handing off agent work while keeping chats,
terminals, diffs, branches, and project state close at hand.

### Brand Personality

Restrained, precise, and quietly confident. The interface should feel fast,
trustworthy, local-first, and calm during long sessions rather than decorative
or attention-seeking.

### Aesthetic Direction

The existing `apps/web` product is the visual and interaction authority. Favor
its compact desktop-tool density, neutral semantic palette, subtle surface
layering, small consistent radii, and purposeful iconography. Support light and
dark themes equally. Do not introduce a parallel visual language, generic AI
gradients, ornamental glass effects, oversized cards, or marketing-page
styling into product surfaces.

### Design Principles

1. Preserve information density without sacrificing hierarchy or legibility.
2. Reuse canonical Web compositions, tokens, copy, and action order across
   platforms; platform-specific code should remain a minimal capability island.
3. Make connection, loading, streaming, disabled, and error states explicit and
   predictable under failure.
4. Treat keyboard flow, accessible names, focus visibility, contrast, and
   reduced motion as product behavior, not optional polish.
5. Validate fidelity against the same real snapshot, route, theme, and viewport;
   target anchor differences of at most 8px and font-size differences of at
   most 2px unless a platform constraint is documented.
